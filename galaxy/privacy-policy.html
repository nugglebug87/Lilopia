Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Applies to the Galaxy Store edition.

LILOPIA for Galaxy Store is published by Unfiltered Ink. This version is an offline pretend-play game designed for players including children.

Game data

No account, real name, email address, phone number, or location access is required. Game progress and preferences are saved in app storage on your device. Core game content is bundled. Internet access is used for Samsung billing, purchase verification and restoration. Product IDs, Samsung purchase and payment identifiers, and transaction status are processed by Samsung for those purposes; they are not exposed to game JavaScript or logged by our app. We do not operate a separate gameplay analytics service. Android app backup is disabled for this edition; device manufacturers may provide their own transfer features.

Purchases and ads

This edition integrates Samsung In-App Purchase for optional permanent expansion packs: Dream Mansion, Beach Vacation and City Life. Availability and local prices come from Galaxy Store. A grown-up must approve purchase access. Other unfinished expansions stay unavailable. Samsung processes payment details under its policies. The game has no advertising, analytics SDK, public chat, or social sharing. Purchase ownership is verified online at startup and when restored; purchases from Google Play do not transfer automatically.

Children and your choices

We do not collect personal information through gameplay. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase progress. Parents and guardians can manage app data using Android settings. If you contact support, your email service and our email service will process the message and address to respond; do not send unnecessary information about a child.

Web pages

Visiting a separately hosted policy or support page may disclose technical request information to its hosting provider. This is distinct from offline gameplay.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Unfilteredinink@gmail.com. We will update this policy before introducing new data collection or services.
