Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

LILOPIA is a pretend-play game published by Unfiltered Ink. We design the game for players of all ages, including children, and aim to minimize data collection.

Information handled by the game

The current game does not require an account, a real name, an email address, a phone number, or location access. Gameplay progress, customization choices, and simulated purchase ownership may be saved locally on your device using browser or app storage. This information is used to keep your game progress and preferences. It is not automatically synchronized across devices. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it may erase locally stored progress.

We do not intentionally collect children's personal information through the current gameplay. The current game has no player chat or social-sharing feature.

Purchases and advertising

The web game includes simulated purchase flows; they do not process real payments. The Android test build has purchases disabled and does not complete real payments. If real in-app purchases are enabled in a future release, payments will be handled by the applicable app store. We will update this policy to describe any purchase identifiers or verification services used before enabling real transactions.

The current game does not display advertisements. If advertising is added later, we will review the advertising provider's data practices, apply child-appropriate safeguards, and update this policy before release. We will not describe the game as ad-free if advertising is enabled.

Hosting and technical information

When you access the web version, its hosting provider may automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request timestamps to deliver the website and maintain security. Such hosting logs are controlled by the hosting provider's practices. We do not currently operate a separate gameplay analytics or tracking system.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell player personal information. Locally saved gameplay data stays on your device unless you or the platform remove it. Technical hosting logs, if any, are retained according to the hosting provider's policies. We may disclose information if required by applicable law.

Children's privacy

LILOPIA is designed to be usable by children without providing personal information. Parents and guardians can help children manage device permissions and clear saved game data. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate.

Your choices

You can reset locally saved progress by clearing the game's site or app storage, subject to the features available on your device. This may permanently erase progress. You can also stop using the game at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting children's information will be addressed before those features launch.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Unfilteredinink@gmail.com.
