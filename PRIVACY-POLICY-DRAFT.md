# LILOPIA privacy policy — draft for owner review

**Not ready to publish: public contact, hosting configuration, retention schedule and release date must be completed.**

Effective date: 10/26/2026

LILOPIA is a pretend-play game for children ages 3–12, published by: Amber @ UnFiltered Ink 
Questions and privacy requests: support@lilopia.com

The game does not require an account, contain public chat, show advertisements, use advertising identifiers, track location, or include analytics or social media SDKs. Characters, household names, preferences and world progress are stored locally on the device. We do not transmit that saved world to a server. You can delete it through Android Settings > Apps > LILOPIA > Storage > Clear storage. Uninstalling also removes local progress. Purchases may be restored separately through Google Play.

When purchases are enabled, optional expansion packs are offered through a grown-up flow and Google's Play payment sheet. When a purchase or restore is checked, the app sends the product ID and Google Play purchase token over encrypted HTTPS to our purchase verification service. That service checks the purchase with Google Play and acknowledges valid payments. We do not receive full payment-card details from Google Play. Google processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy.
