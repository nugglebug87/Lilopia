LILOPIA is a pretend-play game for children ages 3–12, published by [CONFIRMED PUBLIC DEVELOPER NAME]. Questions and privacy requests: [PUBLIC SUPPORT/PRIVACY EMAIL].

The game does not require an account, contain public chat, show advertisements, use advertising identifiers, track location, or include analytics or social media SDKs. Characters, household names, preferences and world progress are stored locally on the device. We do not transmit that saved world to a server. You can delete it through Android Settings > Apps > LILOPIA > Storage > Clear storage. Uninstalling also removes local progress. Purchases may be restored separately through Google Play.

Optional expansion packs are offered through a grown-up flow and Google's Play payment sheet. When a purchase or restore is checked, the app sends the product ID and Google Play purchase token over encrypted HTTPS to our purchase verification service. That service checks the purchase with Google Play and acknowledges valid payments. We do not receive full payment-card details from Google Play. Google processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our verification service stores a one-way hash of the purchase token, the associated product ID and the last verification time to prevent inconsistent transaction processing.
